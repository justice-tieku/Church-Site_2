Our Story

The Cross Passion International is a vibrant youth-based Church pastored by Rev. Festus Agyei Sarfo. Located at the University of Ghana Campus in Accra, Ghana, our church has grown to include multiple branches, each led by dedicated assistant pastors.

Our Mission

Our aim is to reach out to every soul globally, spreading the message of hope, love, and salvation through Jesus Christ. Every member is committed to serving the Lord and fulfilling our divine vision with His guidance.
